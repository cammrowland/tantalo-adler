Practice Areas

Complex Business Disputes

Extensive experience representing clients across the many facets of complex business disputes, before mediators and arbitrators and in state and federal courts. Deep familiarity with the substantive law that governs them: contracts, intellectual property, securities, employment, and consumer class actions.

Media & Entertainment

We have represented film and television studios, production companies, and talent agencies in copyright infringement, idea theft, profit participation, contract, fraud, antitrust, unfair competition, defamation, right of privacy, right of publicity, and First Amendment matters. Beyond litigation, we are consulted on production issues across media and negotiate and draft the related agreements.

Intellectual Property

Patent, trademark, copyright, and trade secret matters in state and federal courts, before the United States Patent and Trademark Office, and before the Trademark Trial and Appeal Board. We also advise outside of litigation and have significant experience negotiating, drafting, and reviewing agreements that license or transfer intellectual property rights.

Legal Malpractice Defense

We have been engaged by many of the most prominent and profitable law firms in the world, including 5 of the top 25 firms in American Lawyer Media's AM Law 100®. That work has covered malpractice defense, defense of fraud and breach of fiduciary duty claims, and complex law firm withdrawals and disqualification proceedings.

Appellate Litigation

Appellate proceedings in state and federal court, with broad experience across appellate procedure: traditional post-judgment appeals, expedited post-injunction appeals, and writ proceedings.

Corporate Transactions

Tantalo & Adler is primarily a litigation and entertainment boutique, but our attorneys handle a range of traditional corporate transactional work: entity formation and maintenance, contract negotiation and review, and non-securities-based transactions.

The Firm

Tantalo & Adler represents clients across the full range of complex commercial litigation and entertainment matters. Our attorneys have been retained by individual entrepreneurs and Fortune 500 corporations alike in fraud, intellectual property, contract, unfair competition, employment, and securities disputes. We have represented film and television studios, producers, distributors, and talent agencies in nearly every facet of media and entertainment litigation, and we have defended some of the world's largest and most prominent law firms.

We are a goal-oriented firm. Our objective is to apply our collective expertise, unique experience, and unrelenting energy to solve your problems and realize your goals, efficiently and cost-effectively. Success is determined through the eyes of our clients.
